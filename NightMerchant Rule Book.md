The year is next Thursday. You have one weekend to make a name for yourself in the gig economy. 

Each player takes the role of a struggling drug Dealer with three 10-hour nights to ply their trade in the city of San Francisco. 

If you're still in the city by sunrise, Lurie's taxing you at 60 percent. 

And if you cross the wrong people, you're dead. 

FACTIONS

***MAGA enjoys the greatest favor with the Cops out of any faction.***

Working with MAGA alienates all other factions.

ISIS is hostile to MAGA, the DSA, and TECH, and is allied with MS13.

**SET UP**  
   
1) Prepare the Phase 2 Delivery board by shuffling each of the **36** tile stacks (**A1** through **F6**) and placing each in its respective neighborhood cell of the grid.  
   
2) Prepare the Phase 1 Market board by shuffling the Goods Deck and dealing 24 Goods from the deck onto the Market board.  
   
When placing goods on the Market, place the good in the row of its respective category, in the rightmost available column of the row.  
(If a row already holds six goods, discard the Good in the rightmost column to the bottom of the Goods deck, push all Goods in the row to the right, and place the new Good in the leftmost column of the row – if this happens, there will be fewer than 24 Goods on the market at the start of the game).  
Once all 24 Goods have been placed on the Market, realign all of the Goods in each row so that the leftmost Good sits in the “Precious” (`<<`) column, and the other Goods in the row occupy the columns immediately to the right of the Good before it. (The “Abundant” (`>>`) column should only have a Good in it if there are six Goods in that category on the market).  
   
3) Shuffle the five **(5)** remaining decks: Celebrities, Cops, Criminals, Missions, and Influence.  
   
4) Distribute to each Player a matching set comprising an SUV Mat, an SUV, a Representative, and two loan tokens.  
   
5) Distribute **12(d)** to each Player from the bank.  
   
6) Deal five **(5)** Mission cards and five **(5)** Influence cards to each Player.  
   
7) Players must choose two **(2)** Mission cards and two **(2)** Influence cards to discard to the bottom of their respective decks.  

8) **Ballot Initiatives (6×3 Board)**: The **first Player to finish discarding** Influence and Mission cards claims **First Pick** on the **Political Climate & End-Game Modifiers** board (six columns: *MAGA*, *ISIS*, *TECH*, *DSA*, *MS13*, and *Gigs*, with three options per column). **At most one (1) initiative may be enacted per column.**  
- **2 Players (4 Initiatives)**: Snake draft **`1st Discarder → Rival → Rival → 1st Discarder`** (the fastest discarder gets **first pick and last pick**!).  
- **3–6 Players (`min(N, 6)` Active Modifiers)**: Each Player enacts **1** modifier in the exact order they finished discarding.  

9) **Normal Game Turn Order & 5th/6th Player Stipend**: Once the Political Climate is set, give the **First Dealer Token** to the Player seated clockwise **after** the First Discarder—so the Player who claimed First Pick on the Political Climate acts **LAST** in normal turn order! In **5–6 Player games**, to offset late Thursday Market position, the **5th Player** in Thursday turn order receives **+1(d)** (**13(d)** total) and the **6th Player** receives **+2(d)** (**14(d)** total) from the bank.  
   
**Combat breaks all ties in the Game (including every Most/Least Political Climate modifier, even when tied at 0!). See COMBAT BETWEEN PLAYERS.**  
 

**PHASES**  
The game takes place over the course of three nights (Thursday, Friday, and Saturday), each night divided into three phases: The Market Phase, the Delivery Phase, and the Clean-Up Phase, followed by the Sunday Morning Reckoning at the end of Saturday.  
   
MARKET PHASE  
The Market Phase takes place in a safe house, represented by the Phase 1 Market board.  
   
In the Market Phase, players take turns buying goods from the Market that they will traffic into the city during Phase 2 to sell. 
   
Starting with the player with the First Dealer Token, and in this specific order:  
   
1) **Source**. Player *may* choose to discard an Influence card in order to rifle through the Goods deck, select a Good, and place it on the Market board in the leftmost column of its respective row, pushing all other Goods in the row to the right.

2) **Buy**. Player *may* then buy one Good from the Market board, paying the Buy Price for the Good listed in the box that corresponds to the column that the Good occupies.  
(i.e. A Good in the “Rare” column can be purchased for its `<` Buy Price).

3) **Replenish**. *If* the Player bought a Good from the Market, place the top card from the Goods Deck onto the Market board in the leftmost column of its respective category, pushing all other Goods in the row to the right (any Goods pushed off the Market are moved to the bottom of the Goods deck).

4) **Pass**. When a Player has completed their turn, play passes to the next Player in clockwise order.

5) **End Market Phase**. When a round passes with all players neglecting to buy a good from the Market, the Market Phase is over, and play proceeds to the Delivery Phase.  
**When the Market Phase ends, pass the First Player Token clockwise.**  
(If you are familiar with poker, this is the same; play proceeds once there has been a full round of play without buys.)  
(Discarding an Influence Card to source a Good onto the Market does not count as a Buy.)  
 

DELIVERY PHASE  
Players will spend up to 10 turns driving around SF in the hope of completing missions and selling goods for a profit.  
   
Remember that this is a night delivery – you are racing against sunrise. Any Players still in SF at the end of the 10th turn will pay a hefty fine!  
   
I) **Set the Clock**. Place the turn clock token in the First Turn section of the clock board (8:00 P.M. – 9:00 P.M.).  
II) **Initial Scout**. Beginning with the Player with the First Player Token, each Player may look at the top tile of one of the tile stacks and put it back.  
III) **Start your engines.** Continuing in turn order, each Player selects a point of entry (any of the bridges or highways: **B1**, **F1**, **F5**, **D6**, or **A5**). Entering SF counts as your Move on Turn 1 (8:00 P.M.). You will reveal that tile and resolve its encounter on your first turn.  
   
Starting with the Player with the First Dealer Token:  
   
1) **Trash a tile or card**. Player *may* elect to discard an Influence Card in order to move the top exposed or unexposed tile to the bottom of its stack **or** the top card of any deck (Celebrities, Criminals, Cops, Missions, Influence, Goods) to the bottom of its deck.

2) **Move**. Player *may* move once in any available direction (including exiting SF off an open perimeter edge):  
Green: can pass through.  
Red: impassable.  
Protesters Icon: passable, but triggers Protesters table encounter.  
Open Mic Icon: passable, but triggers Open Mic table encounter.  
Schizophrenic Icon: passable, but triggers Schizophrenic table encounter.  
Cops Icon: passable, but triggers Cops Deck Encounter.  
Criminals Icon: passable, but triggers Criminals Deck Encounter.  
Celebrities Icon: passable, but triggers Celebrities Deck Encounter.  
2(d): Must pay **2 denarii** (or listed amount) in order to pass through this edge.  
*Complete Edge Encounters before moving to or revealing the next tile.*

3) **Reveal Tile**. Unless the cell being entered has a revealed tile with a **⤹** (persistent) icon, reveal the top unrevealed tile of the tile stack (moving the presently revealed tile to the bottom of the tile stack if appropriate). Then place Player’s SUV on the new cell.

4) **Complete Mission(s)**. Player *may* fulfill any missions that Player has that are relevant to the occupied cell.  
For Delivery Missions: Items can be picked up at this cell by moving the mission cargo onto the Player’s SUV mat. Items can be delivered at this cell immediately upon arrival (before any Step 5 Tile Encounter) by moving the mission card to the bottom of the Missions Deck and claiming its rewards.  
For Sell Missions: Player may complete the mission during Step 7 (Transact) by selling **one (1)** matching Good—or any number of non-fenced **Grain** cards if it is a Grain Sell Mission—subject to the price modifier indicated on the Mission Card (e.g., if the mission is to “Sell Cheap Wine at `+` in C3”, and if the market scarcity for Wine is “Common/`>`”, then when Player arrives at C3, Player can sell 1 Cheap Wine for the amount listed in the “Uncommon/`=`” Sell Price column for that wine).

5) **Trigger Encounter**. If the revealed Tile has an encounter icon in the central field, trigger the appropriate Deck or Table encounter indicated by the icon(s).

6) **Refresh**. If the revealed Tile has a **⤿** icon, then move it to the bottom of the Tile stack after resolving the encounter, reveal the next Tile in the stack, and continue from Step 5.

7) **Transact**. Player may make one Sale, Purchase, or Gamble:  
- **Sell (1 Good per Hour — or Bulk Grain!)**: Player may sell **at most one (1) Good card** in a category printed in the white box on the revealed tile (or authorized by an active Sell Mission at this cell)—**EXCEPT for non-fenced Grain**, where a single hourly Transaction may sell **any number (or all!) of the Player’s Grain cards simultaneously** at the current Grain scarcity modifier. The Scarcity Modifier for the category affects the sell price for the Good(s) relative to present market scarcity. *(Fenced Encounter Mission cargo is always sold 1 card at a time.)*  
- **Purchase**: (On any of the three central Forum cells: **C3**, **D3**, **D4**) Player can purchase one good from the Market subject to the Scarcity Modifier listed on the tile.  
- **Gamble**: If the revealed tile has a Gambling/Dice icon, then the Player can elect to spend their transaction making a single Gamble of up to **20(d)**. See TABLE ENCOUNTERS – Gambling.

8) **Free Scout** – Player may look at the top Tile of any cell or the top Card of any of the six Decks and then put the tile or card back on the top of its respective stack or deck.

9) **Advance Turn** – Once all Players have acted, advance the Clock one hour and pass the First Player Token clockwise. Repeat until Sunrise (the end of Turn 10) or until all SUVs have left Rome.  
 

CLEAN-UP PHASE  
Clean-Up Phase is used to prepare for the following night.

1) **Pay Fines**. Any Players still in Rome pay a daybreak penalty. This penalty is halved if the Player is occupying a Gate (“Via”/entry point) Tile (**B1, E1, F3, F6, B6, A4**). See DAWN.

2) **Clean Up Rome**. Move all exposed tiles to the bottom of their respective stack.

3) **Buy Influence**. In turn order, Players can buy Influence cards with cash. The price of each card depends on the number of Influence Cards currently in Player’s hand (including any face-down Schizophrenic cards):  
0–1 Cards: **2(d)**  
2–3 Cards: **4(d)**  
4 Cards: **6(d)**  
5+ Cards: **8(d)**  
    	   
4) **Take Out & Repay Loans.** At the end of Thursday and Friday, Players have the option of taking out loans (accruing 50% compound interest per night):  
   
**Thursday Loan**: Receive **20(d)** at the end of Thursday.  
- Repay at end of Friday: **30(d)**  
- Repay at end of Saturday / Deducted at Sunday Reckoning: **45(d)**  
   
**Friday Loan**: Receive **40(d)** at the end of Friday.  
- Repay at end of Saturday / Deducted at Sunday Reckoning: **60(d)**  
   
5) **Pay Your Henchmen.** Players must pay any henchmen they wish to keep an amount equal to what their hiring cost would be if they were placed in the first exposed slot on the right of the market board (i.e. if there are three henchmen on the market, the Pay price is the Uncommon/`=` Hire price of the henchman; if there are four henchmen on the market, the Pay price is the Common/`>` Hire price of the henchman).

6) **Next Night**. Pass the First Player Token clockwise and advance to the next night (or proceed to Sunday Reckoning after Saturday). *(In the digital edition, during Saturday Clean-Up Players may also toggle whether their Driver is authorized to expend Combat Influence cards during Sunday Reckoning PvP tie-breakers.)*  
 

**ENCOUNTERS**  
Resolve an Encounter the moment Player occupies a tile with an Encounter icon in the center or passes through an Edge with an Encounter Icon on it.  
After resolving the Encounter, Player may complete movement and transactions.  
   
For **Deck Encounters**: Player draws the top card from the corresponding Deck Type.  
For **Table Encounters**: Player rolls a ® and consults the corresponding table.  
   
DECK ENCOUNTERS  
When a Deck Encounter is triggered, Player does the following:  
1) **Draw**. Reveal the top card of the corresponding deck.  
2) **Trigger Reaction**. Consult the highlighted bars in the middle of the card to see how the encountered character reacts to Player:  
i) **Identify Character’s Faction**. Indicated by the color of the highlighted bars and portrait outline, the faction gem icon, and the character bio.  
ii) **Calculate Player’s Favor (Uncapped)**. Count the number of positive Favor gems that Player has collected for the corresponding Faction and subtract the number of negated gems collected for that Faction (plus any temporary Favor modifier). **Faction Favor is uncapped** and can climb to `±5` or higher!  
iii) **Determine Character’s Reaction.** Compare Player’s Favor to the highlighted threshold bars on the encounter card.  
iv) **Apply Reaction**. Possible reactions include:  
a) **Combat Icon**: Initiate combat. *(Digital adaptation note: Players cannot voluntarily initiate combat against non-threatening encounters unless unlocked by a combat Mission or the ‘Casus Belli’ Influence card.)*  
b) **!! (Demand)**: Player must fulfill the demand or initiate combat. If Player is only partially able to fulfill the demand, Player can avoid combat by satisfying the demand to the greatest extent possible (taking Hostile Favor consequences). If Player has none of the thing being demanded (when noted on card), ignore the demand completely.  
c) **! (Request)**: Player must fulfill the request or take Hostile Favor consequences.  
d) **? (Offer)**: Player may partake in the offered transaction. This does not consume the Player’s transaction for the turn.  
e) **Quest / Mission**: Character demands or offers a Mission.  

DECK (T)YPES  
**Criminals** – [Gray] – (B) Bandits, conspirators, and nocturnal fixers (likely to rob or kill you).  
**Celebrities** – [Saffron] – (A) Aristocrats, senators, and courtiers (likely to send you on an errand).  
**Cops** – [Madder] – (L) Soldiers, prefects, and city watchmen (likely to demand a bribe).  
   
TABLE ENCOUNTERS  
**Protesters**:  
®:  
0: Takes all of Player’s Wine  
I: Takes all of Player’s Grain  
II: Takes Player’s most expensive cargo  
III: Clamors noisily (no loss)  

**Schizophrenic**: Player draws the top Missions Card as a mandatory Schizophrenic Mission and places ® Influence cards face down under it. Player collects those Influence cards when the goal is satisfied. Face-down Schizophrenic cards count as “in Player’s hand” when calculating the price of buying Influence Cards in Clean-Up.  

**Open Mic**:  
®:  
0: Lose all Friendly Consequence Favor  
I: Lose all Hostile Consequence Favor  
II: Lose this and your next turn (flip SUV over)  
III: Draw ® Influence Cards  

ADDICTION   
Every Dealer (player character) is a reprobate open mic comic and recovering cocaine addict. 

If a Dealer enters combat while in possession of Uppers, that Dealer will consume those Uppers and unleash the effects they have on combat, for better or for worse, whether or not the player desires for the Dealer to do so. 

Similarly, if the Dealer encounters an open mic, the Dealer will stop in for a set, whether or not the player desires for the Dealer to waste time or reputation to do so.

EDGES  
[Green] Can pass through.  
[Red] Cannot pass through.  
**2(d)** Pay 2 denarii to pass through.  
[Icon] Resolve Encounter to pass through.  
   
STADIUM GAMBLING  
If the Gambling symbol is present on the revealed tile, Player can elect to spend their turn’s Transaction gambling:  
1) Player places a cash wager on top of the tile (**maximum 20(d) per gambling action**).  
2) Player rolls ® (or `2®` if *Match Fixing* is played).  
3) Player receives back cash equal to the total rolled times the bet:  
0: **Lose** – Wager goes to the bank.  
I: **Break even** – Wager is returned (`1×`).  
II: **Double Up** – Receive `2×` wager (`+1×` net profit).  
III: **Triple Up** – Receive `3×` wager (`+2×` net profit).  
*(The expected value of 1® is 1.167×; with Match Fixing’s 2®, up to a 6× payout is possible on the 20(d) maximum wager.)*  
   
**MISSIONS**  
A Player can have a **Maximum of five (5) active missions**. There are three types of mission assignments:

1) **Starting Missions**. At the beginning of the game, Players receive five distinct missions and pick three to keep. These missions can be failed voluntarily (by canceling or replacing them when full). There is no Favor penalty for failing Starting Missions *(unless **Bonded Couriers** is active, which fines **-10(d)** on every failed or unfinished mission)*, **and Goods picked up for Starting Mission delivery quests cannot be sold** (if abandoned, the cargo is discarded for nothing, never fenced).

2) **Deck Encounter Missions**. A character encountered in Rome may give the Player a mission. Goods picked up for Deck Encounter delivery missions *can* be fenced to an interested buyer for the Fenced Sell value on the card, which immediately fails the mission and triggers **Hostile Favor Consequences** from the patron *(unless **Smuggler’s Fence** is active, in which case fenced cargo sells **1 Scarcity step higher** and does **not** count as a Failed Mission, though Patron Hostile Favor still applies)*. If a Deck Encounter Mission is failed for any reason (including remaining unfinished at the end of Saturday!), Player receives the **Hostile Favor Consequences** from the patron.  

3) **Schizophrenic Missions**. Mandatory and cannot be failed voluntarily (unless holding 6 Schizophrenic missions simultaneously). Goods picked up for Schizophrenic delivery missions cannot be sold, though they can be stolen.  

4) **Special Imperial Combat Missions (*Regicide* & *Regime Change*)**. Two high-stakes Encounter Missions carry an **“any Player”** clause:  
- **Regicide**: Completed if **any Player** defeats Emperor **Caracalla** in combat while you hold *Regicide*. Completing *Regicide* **immediately ends the game in an instant assassination victory** for the Player holding *Regicide*!  
- **Regime Change**: Completed if **any Player** defeats Co-Emperor **Geta** in combat while you hold *Regime Change*, awarding **100(d)** plus `+2 Rubies` and `+1 Diamond` while play continues normally.  

DEMANDS & COMBAT  
COMBAT  
1) Player takes (Hostile) Favor Consequences from the Encounter Card.  
2) Generate Player’s CP:  
- Player’s Driver has `1®` CP  
- `+` CP from Henchmen  
- `+` CP from Weapons *(Note: crated **Loricae** body armor carries **0 CP** in sudden street ambushes—too slow to strap on mid-attack, offset by its lucrative sell-to-buy margin!)*  
- `+` Mandatory Uppers in SUV (must roll all Uppers in combat!)  
- `+` `+1 CP` per positive Caracalla Ruby if **Army Pay Hike** is active  
3) Opponent rolls `(#)®` CP (unless card formula states otherwise; `+2 CP` to all `Cops` NPCs if **Army Pay Hike** is active; *Distraction* deducts `1®` positive die from the target’s pool prior to rolling).  
4) All ® on both sides are rolled simultaneously so post-roll Influence cards (*Twist Of Fate*, *Flying Chamber Pot*, *Gladiator’s Intervention*) may modify the dice.  
5) If **Player’s CP ≥ Opponent’s CP**: **Player wins**, collects Combat Rewards (if any), and Player’s turn proceeds.  
Otherwise, **Opponent wins**:  
a) Player suffers `1` Wound (W) for each point of CP difference (after shield/helmet wound-ignore rolls). Henchmen absorb wounds first (Player chooses which Henchman dies if holding multiple; dead Henchmen return to the bottom of the Market deck), and the Driver must survive if possible.  
b) Player’s turn ends.  
c) **If Player initiated combat by refusing a Demand, Player must still fulfill the refused Demand** (to the greatest extent possible from remaining cash/goods/influence).  
   
DEATH  
If the Driver dies, Player rolls ®:  
0: **Yep, dead.** Player loses all Goods, ends turn; Player’s night is over.  
I: **Left for dead.** Player loses all Goods, night continues.  
II: **Beaten and mugged.** Player loses all Cash, night continues.  
III: **Begged for mercy.** Player loses all Influence Cards, night continues.  
   
DAWN  
At the end of the 10th turn, any Player whose suv is still in the city pays a curfew fine:
- **Thursday**: **12(d)** (`6(d)` on a Gate)  
- **Friday**: **20(d)** (`10(d)` on a Gate)  
- **Saturday**: **32(d)** (`16(d)` on a Gate)  

If the entire fine cannot be covered in Cash, Player must pay the remainder in kind, forfeiting Goods with enough Uncommon (`=`) Sell Price value to satisfy the amount owed.

During Setup, Players draft active modifiers from the **6×3 Political Climate Board** (4 modifiers in a 2-Player game; `min(N, 6)` modifiers in 3–6 Player games; max 1 option per column):

After Saturday Clean-Up concludes, December 211 arrives and each Player’s final score in **Denarii (d)** is settled in strict order:
1) **Unfinished Missions**: Any incomplete Missions fail automatically (counting as Failed Missions, fining **-10(d)** each if *Bonded Couriers* is active, and applying **Hostile Favor Consequences** for incomplete Deck Encounter Missions).
2) **Unsold Goods Liquidation**: Remaining non-mission Goods in a Player’s SUV liquidate at their **Abundant (`>>`) Sell Price**.
3) **Unpaid Loans**: Deduct **45(d)** for an unpaid Thursday Loan and **60(d)** for an unpaid Friday Loan.
4) ** Political Climate Settlement**:
   - **Step 4a — Elimination FIRST** (*Imperial Proscription*, if active): The Player with **Least** Caracalla Favor is **eliminated immediately** before any other Political Climate awards or penalties resolve. Eliminated Players rank below all survivors, cannot win the game, and are **excluded from all subsequent Most/Least awards, penalties, steals, and percentage bonuses**!
   - **Step 4b — Flat Gem/Mission Reckoning & Penalties**: Among surviving Players, resolve enacted flat per-gem/per-mission modifiers and Least penalties (*Praetorian Backlash*, *Brother’s Grudge*, *Jealous Brothers*, *Court Neutrality*, *Smuggled Dowry*, *Old Senate Purse*, *Choose a Side*, *Master Purveyor*, *Guild Ledger*).
   - **Step 4c — Wealth Steal & Percentage Patronage**: Among surviving Players, resolve *Exile’s Informants* (`15(d)` stolen from the wealthiest surviving rival), followed by *Imperial Patronage* (`+20%` of positive wealth, rounded to nearest Denarius).

The surviving Merchant with the highest final Denarii total wins!

FAVOR SUMMARY  
- **Uncapped**: Faction Favor has **no cap** (`±5` or higher is possible).  
- **(Ruby) – Caracalla (Bassianus)**: Senior Emperor; loved by mother and troops; otherwise feared and despised.  
- **(Emerald) – Geta**: Junior Emperor; loved by mother and sycophants; murdered at year’s end.  
- **(Sapphire) – Plautilla**: Banished Empress conspiring in Rome by night; hated by Severans, loved by Rome’s underworld (*Criminals*).  
- **(Amethyst) – Aurelius**: Old Senatorial Order; revered by aristocrats (*Celebrities*), distrusted by the emperors.  
- **(Diamond) – Domna**: Emperors’ Mother; loves both Severan sons, hostile to their rivals.  
   
COMBAT BETWEEN PLAYERS  
**All ties in the game**—including every **Most** or **Least** Political Climate award (even when Players are tied at `0`!), tied wealthiest rivals, and Final Score ties at Sunday Reckoning—are broken by **Combat between the tied Players**, using Drivers (`1®`), Henchmen, Weapons, mandatory Exotic Animals, combat Influence cards, and `+1 CP` per positive Ruby if *Army Pay Hike* is active.  
- **Pre-Roll vs. Post-Roll Combat Influence**: Pre-roll cards (*Mercenary Ambush* `+1®`, *Distraction* deducting `1®` positive die from the opponent’s pool prior to rolling) apply before dice are cast; all ® on both sides roll simultaneously; and reactive post-roll cards (*Twist Of Fate* rerolling a low positive die, a high enemy die, or a negative Exotic Animal die; *Flying Chamber Pot* zeroing an opponent’s highest die; *Gladiator’s Intervention* `+3 CP`) may be played when they can overturn a deficit or tie. *(In the digital edition, Players may opt out of auto-spending Combat Influence cards during Sunday Reckoning tie-breakers during Saturday Clean-Up.)*  
- **Strictly One Recipient**: Every Most/Least bonus or penalty is awarded to **strictly one Player**. For **Most** awards, the Combat **winner** claims the bonus; for **Least** penalties or elimination, the Combat winner escapes and the Combat **loser** suffers the penalty!  
- **No Draws**: If a Combat between Players ends in a draw, repeat Combat (any one-shot Influence cards or consumable Weapons used in a previous round remain exhausted) until one side wins by at least **1 CP**!

## SYMBOL & ICON REFERENCE

### Goods & Henchmen Card Symbols
- **Bold Green `+𐆖` (Sell / Fence Price Row)**: The top row on a Good Card’s price table. Indicates the **Denarii (`𐆖`) you receive** when selling that Good in Rome (or Fencing Encounter Mission Cargo) at each Scarcity column (`<<` to `>>`).
- **Italic Red `-𐆖` (Buy / Hire Price Row)**: The bottom row on a Good or Henchman Card’s price table. Indicates the **Denarii (`𐆖`) you pay** to purchase that Good at Portus Ostiae (or an Imperial Forum) or to hire/retain a Henchman.
- **Denarius (`𐆖` / `(d)`)**: Roman silver coin (`U+10196`) used for all purchases, sales, road tolls, Stadium wagers, Henchman upkeep, and final victory scoring.
- **`<<`  `<`  `=`  `>`  `>>` (5 Scarcity Columns)**:
  - `<<` **Precious** (`0` cards remaining on that category’s Ostia Market track)
  - `<` **Rare** (`1` card remaining)
  - `=` **Uncommon** (`2–3` cards remaining)
  - `>` **Common** (`4` cards remaining)
  - `>>` **Abundant** (`5–6` cards remaining)
- **`■` (Cargo Space Cubes)**: Brown squares indicating how many of your SUV Mat’s **6 Cargo blocks (`6■`)** a Good occupies (`1■`, `2■`, or `3■`).
- **`▲` (Henchman Seat Triangles)**: Gold triangles indicating how many of your SUV Mat’s **2 Henchman seats (`2▲`)** a hired Henchman occupies (`1▲` or `2▲`).

### Rome Tile, Action, & Encounter Icons
- **`(M)` (Blue Move Circle)**: Represents **1 SUV Movement** step along an open street edge.
- **`(T)` (Gold Transaction Circle)**: Represents **1 hourly Transaction** (Sell 1 Good, Bulk-Sell all Grain, Buy 1 Forum Good, or Gamble at a Stadium).
- **`(R)` / `®` (Roman Numeral Die)**: 6-sided custom Roman die with faces **`0, 0, I, I, II, III`** (average roll = `1.0`).
- **`++`  `+`  `=`  `-`  `--` (Tile Buyer & Forum Modifiers)**: Shifts the active Market Scarcity column 1 or 2 steps left toward `<<` Precious (`+` / `++`) or right toward `>>` Abundant (`-` / `--`).
- **Persistent (`⤹`) & Refresh (`↻` / `⤿`) Tile Icons**: Persistent landmark tiles remain face-up across nights; Refresh tiles discard after their encounter resolves and immediately reveal the next tile.
- **Encounter Icons**:
  - **Celebrities** (*Senatorial Bust*): Draws from the Celebrities deck (aligned with Aurelius, Domna, or Geta).
  - **Cops** (*Roman Helmet*): Draws from the Cops deck (heavily aligned with Caracalla).
  - **Criminals** (*Dagger / 3× Daggers*): Draws 1 (or 3) cards from the Criminals underworld deck (often aligned with Plautilla).
  - **Protesters** (*Torch & Pitchfork*): Roll `1(R)` (`0`: lose Wine; `I`: lose Grain; `II`: lose costliest Good; `III`: safe).
  - **Open Mic** (*Comedy/Tragedy Masks*): Roll `1(R)` (`0`: lose positive Favor; `I`: lose negative Favor; `II`: lose rest of turn & next turn; `III`: draw `1(R)` Influence).
  - **Schizophrenic** (*Augur’s Hand*): Draws a mandatory Schizophrenic Mission and seals `1(R)` face-down Influence cards beneath it until fulfilled.
  - **Stadium Dice**: Spend your hourly Transaction to wager `1(d)–20(d)` and roll `1(R)` to win `Wager × Roll`.
