The year is next Thursday. You have one weekend to make a name for yourself in the gig economy. 

Each player takes the role of a struggling drug Dealer with three 10-hour nights to ply their trade in the city of San Francisco. 

If you're still in the city by sunrise, Lurie's taxing you at 60 percent. 

And if you cross the wrong people, you're dead. 

FACTIONS  
   
**MAGA** – RUBY – Donald J. Trump would become known to history as the first emperor of the **MAGA** dynasty.  
***MAGA enjoys the greatest favor with the Cops out of any faction.***  
MAGA is hostile to MS13 and ISIS, allied with TECH, and passively hostile to the DSA.  
Working with MAGA alienates all other factions.  
   
**ISIS** – EMERALD – The task of rebuilding ISIS has fallen to Jared Kushner.  
ISIS is hostile to MAGA, the DSA, and TECH, and is allied with MS13.  
ISIS is the smallest, most hostile faction.  
   
**TECH** – DIAMOND – Mark Zuckerberg leads a cabal of Galtian techno-founders and AI billionaires.  
TECH is ambivalent to MAGA, hostile to the DSA, and generally likes it when the player has hostile interactions with the other factions.  
TECH is wealthy and generous.  
   
**DSA** – SAPPHIRE – Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez is the lead figurehead of the DSA, a coalition of progressive organizers, artists, and activists, whether she admits to it or not.  
DSA is most hostile to MAGA, averse to TECH, and sometimes allied with ISIS and MS13.  
DSA is strongest among the Celebrities.  
   
**MS13** – AMETHYST – Nayib Bukele brought his officers to the city claiming that he was coming to help solve the gang violence crisis.  
***MS13 enjoys the most favor with the Criminals.***  
MS13 is hostile to MAGA, but mostly indifferent to the other factions.


**SET UP**  
   
1) Prepare the Phase 2 Delivery board by shuffling each of the **36** tile stacks (**A1** through **F6**) and placing each in its respective neighborhood cell of the San Francisco grid.  
   
2) Prepare the Phase 1 Safe House Market board by shuffling the Goods Deck (**112 cards**: 16 Weed, 16 Party, 24 Uppers [12 Pill Uppers + 12 Powder Uppers], 24 Opiates [12 Street Opiates + 12 Pharma Opiates], 16 Weapons, and 16 Henchmen) and dealing 24 Goods from the deck onto the Market board.  
   
When placing goods on the Market, place the good in the row of its respective category, in the rightmost available column of the row.  
(If a row already holds six goods, discard the Good in the rightmost column to the bottom of the Goods deck, push all Goods in the row to the right, and place the new Good in the leftmost column of the row – if this happens, there will be fewer than 24 Goods on the market at the start of the game).  
Once all 24 Goods have been placed on the Market, realign all of the Goods in each row so that the leftmost Good sits in the “Precious” (`<<`) column, and the other Goods in the row occupy the columns immediately to the right of the Good before it. (The “Abundant” (`>>`) column should only have a Good in it if there are six Goods in that category on the market).  
   
3) Shuffle the five **(5)** remaining decks: Celebrities (30), Cops (30), Criminals (29), Jobs (56), and Influence (43).  
   
4) Distribute to each Player a matching set comprising an SUV Mat, an SUV token, a Dealer token, and two loan tokens.  
   
5) Distribute **₿1.2** (`12` tenth-BTC units) to each Player from the bank.  
   
6) Deal five **(5)** Job cards and five **(5)** Influence cards to each Player.  
   
7) Players must choose two **(2)** Job cards and two **(2)** Influence cards to discard to the bottom of their respective decks.  

8) **Ballot Initiatives (6×3 Board)**: The **first Player to finish discarding** Influence and Job cards claims **First Pick** on the **Ballot Initiatives** board (six columns: *MAGA*, *ISIS*, *TECH*, *DSA*, *MS13*, and *Gigs*, with three options per column). **At most one (1) initiative may be enacted per column.**  
- **2 Players (4 Initiatives)**: Snake draft **`1st Discarder → Rival → Rival → 1st Discarder`** (the fastest discarder gets **first pick and last pick**!).  
- **3–6 Players (`min(N, 6)` Active Initiatives)**: Each Player enacts **1** initiative in the exact order they finished discarding.  

9) **Normal Game Turn Order & 5th/6th Player Stipend**: Once the Ballot Initiatives are set, give the **First Dealer Token** to the Player seated clockwise **after** the First Discarder—so the Player who claimed First Pick on the Ballot Initiatives acts **LAST** in normal turn order! In **5–6 Player games**, to offset late Thursday Market position, the **5th Player** in Thursday turn order receives **+₿0.1** (**₿1.3** total) and the **6th Player** receives **+₿0.2** (**₿1.4** total) from the bank.  
   
**Combat breaks all ties in the Game (including every Most/Least Ballot Initiative modifier, even when tied at 0!). See COMBAT BETWEEN PLAYERS.**  
 

**PHASES**  
The game takes place over the course of three nights (Thursday, Friday, and Saturday), each night divided into three phases: The Market Phase, the Delivery Phase, and the Clean-Up Phase, followed by the **Sunday Morning Reckoning** at the end of Saturday.  
   
MARKET PHASE  
The Market Phase takes place in a safe house motel room, represented by the Phase 1 Safe House Market board (an open suitcase on a motel bed).  
   
In the Market Phase, players take turns buying goods and hiring henchmen from the Market that they will traffic into San Francisco during Phase 2.  
   
Starting with the player with the First Dealer Token, and in this specific order:  
   
1) **Source**. Player *may* choose to discard an Influence card in order to rifle through the Goods deck, select a Good, and place it on the Market board in the leftmost column of its respective row, pushing all other Goods in the row to the right.

2) **Buy / Hire**. Player *may* then buy one Good (or hire one Henchman) from the Market board, paying the Wholesale Price (`-₿`) listed in the box that corresponds to the column that the card occupies.  
(i.e. A Good in the “Rare” column can be purchased for its `<` Wholesale Price).

3) **Replenish**. *If* the Player bought a Good from the Market, place the top card from the Goods Deck onto the Market board in the leftmost column of its respective category, pushing all other Goods in the row to the right (any Goods pushed off the Market are moved to the bottom of the Goods deck).

4) **Pass**. When a Player has completed their turn, play passes to the next Player in clockwise order.

5) **End Market Phase**. When a round passes with all players neglecting to buy a good from the Market, the Market Phase is over, and play proceeds to the Delivery Phase.  
**When the Market Phase ends, pass the First Dealer Token clockwise.**  
(If you are familiar with poker, this is the same; play proceeds once there has been a full round of play without buys.)  
(Discarding an Influence Card to source a Good onto the Market does not count as a Buy.)  
 

DELIVERY PHASE  
Players will spend up to 10 hourly turns driving around San Francisco in the hope of completing jobs and selling goods for a profit.  
   
Remember that this is a night delivery – you are racing against sunrise. Any Players still in San Francisco at the end of the 10th turn (5:00 A.M.) will pay a hefty curfew fine!  
   
I) **Set the Clock**. Place the turn clock token in the First Turn section of the parking meter clock board (8:00 P.M. – 9:00 P.M.).  
II) **Initial Scout**. Beginning with the Player with the First Dealer Token, each Player may look at the top tile of one of the tile stacks (or the top card of any deck) and put it back.  
III) **Start your engines.** Continuing in turn order, each Player selects one of the **5 Entry Points** into San Francisco: **B1** (Presidio / Golden Gate Bridge), **F1** (Treasure Island / Bay Bridge), **F5** (Bayview / US-101), **D6** (Excelsior / I-280), or **A5** (Merced / Route 1). Entering SF counts as your Move on Turn 1 (8:00 P.M.). You will reveal that tile and resolve its encounter on your first turn.  
   
Starting with the Player with the First Dealer Token:  
   
1) **Trash a tile or card**. Player *may* elect to discard an Influence Card in order to move the top exposed or unexposed tile of any neighborhood to the bottom of its stack **or** the top card of any of the 6 decks (Celebrities, Criminals, Cops, Goods, Jobs, Influence) to the bottom of its deck.

2) **Move**. Player *may* move once in any available direction (including exiting SF off an open perimeter edge):  
- **Green**: can pass through.  
- **Red**: impassable (unless *Shortcut* is played).  
- **Protesters Icon**: passable, but triggers Protesters table encounter.  
- **Open Mic Icon**: passable, but triggers Open Mic table encounter.  
- **Schizophrenic Icon**: passable, but triggers Schizophrenic table encounter.  
- **Cops Icon**: passable, but triggers Cops Deck Encounter (`1–4×`).  
- **Criminals Icon**: passable, but triggers Criminals Deck Encounter (`1–4×`).  
- **Celebrities Icon**: passable, but triggers Celebrities Deck Encounter (`1×`).  
- **₿0.2 Toll**: Must pay **₿0.2** (or listed toll amount) in order to pass through this edge.  
*Complete Edge Encounters before moving to or revealing the next tile.*

3) **Reveal Tile**. Unless the cell being entered has a revealed tile with a **⤹** (persistent) icon, reveal the top unrevealed tile of the tile stack (moving the presently revealed tile to the bottom of the tile stack if appropriate). Then place Player’s SUV on the new cell.

4) **Complete Job(s)**. Player *may* fulfill any Jobs that Player has that are relevant to the occupied cell.  
- **For Delivery Jobs**: Cargo can be picked up at this cell by moving the job cargo onto the Player’s SUV mat. Cargo can be delivered at this cell immediately upon arrival (before any Step 5 Tile Encounter) by moving the job card to the bottom of the Jobs Deck and claiming its rewards.  
- **For Sell Jobs**: Player may complete the job during Step 7 (Transact) by selling **one (1)** matching Good—or any number of non-fenced **Weed** cards if it is a Weed Sell Job—subject to the price modifier indicated on the Job Card (e.g., if the job is to “Sell Street Opiates at `+` in C3”, and if the market scarcity for Opiates is “Common/`>`”, then when Player arrives at C3, Player can sell 1 Street Opiates card for the amount listed in the “Uncommon/`=`” Retail Price `+₿` column).

5) **Trigger Encounter**. If the revealed Tile has an encounter icon in the central field, trigger the appropriate Deck or Table encounter indicated by the icon(s).

6) **Refresh**. If the revealed Tile has a **⤿** icon, then move it to the bottom of the Tile stack after resolving the encounter, reveal the next Tile in the stack, and continue from Step 5.

7) **Transact**. Player may make one Sale, Purchase, or Gamble:  
- **Sell (1 Good per Hour — or Bulk Weed!)**: Player may sell **at most one (1) Good card** in a category printed in the buyer box on the revealed tile (or authorized by an active Sell Job at this cell)—**EXCEPT for non-fenced Weed**, where a single hourly Transaction may sell **any number (or all!) of the Player’s Weed cards simultaneously** at the current Weed scarcity modifier. The Scarcity Modifier for the category shifts the Retail Price (`+₿`) column relative to present market scarcity. *(Fenced Encounter Job cargo is always sold 1 card at a time.)*  
- **Purchase**: On any of the three central Open-Air Market cells (**C3 Civic Center**, **D3 Tenderloin**, **D4 Mission**), Player can purchase one Good from the Safe House Market subject to the Scarcity Modifier listed on the tile.  
- **Gamble**: If the revealed tile has a Gambling/Dice icon, then the Player can elect to spend their transaction making a single Gamble of up to **₿2.0**. See TABLE ENCOUNTERS – Gambling.

8) **Free Scout** – Player may look at the top Tile of any cell or the top Card of any of the six Decks and then put the tile or card back on the top of its respective stack or deck.

9) **Advance Turn** – Once all Players have acted, advance the Clock one hour and pass the First Dealer Token clockwise. Repeat until Sunrise (the end of Turn 10) or until all SUVs have exited San Francisco.  
 

CLEAN-UP PHASE  
Clean-Up Phase is used to prepare for the following night.

1) **Pay Curfew Fines**. Any Players still in San Francisco pay a sunrise curfew penalty. This penalty is halved if the Player is occupying one of the **5 Entry Point** Tiles (**B1, F1, F5, D6, A5**). See DAWN.

2) **Clean Up San Francisco**. Move all non-persistent exposed tiles to the bottom of their respective stack.

3) **Buy Influence**. In turn order, Players can buy Influence cards with Bitcoin. The price of each card depends on the number of Influence Cards currently in Player’s hand (including any face-down Schizophrenic cards):  
- 0–1 Cards: **₿0.2**  
- 2–3 Cards: **₿0.4**  
- 4 Cards: **₿0.6**  
- 5+ Cards: **₿0.8**  
    	   
4) **Take Out & Repay Loans.** At the end of Thursday and Friday, Players have the option of taking out loans (accruing 50% compound interest per night):  
   
**Thursday Loan**: Receive **₿2.0** at the end of Thursday.  
- Repay at end of Friday: **₿3.0**  
- Repay at end of Saturday / Deducted at Sunday Morning Reckoning: **₿4.5**  
   
**Friday Loan**: Receive **₿4.0** at the end of Friday.  
- Repay at end of Saturday / Deducted at Sunday Morning Reckoning: **₿6.0**  
   
5) **Pay Your Henchmen.** Players must pay any henchmen they wish to keep an amount equal to what their hiring cost would be if they were placed in the first exposed slot on the right of the market board (i.e. if there are three henchmen on the market, the Salary price is the Uncommon/`=` Hire price of the henchman; if there are four henchmen on the market, the Salary price is the Common/`>` Hire price of the henchman).

6) **Next Night**. Pass the First Dealer Token clockwise and advance to the next night (or proceed to the **Sunday Morning Reckoning** after Saturday). *(In the digital edition, during Saturday Clean-Up Players may also toggle whether their Driver is authorized to expend Combat Influence cards during Sunday Morning Reckoning PvP tie-breakers.)*  
 

**ENCOUNTERS**  
Resolve an Encounter the moment Player occupies a tile with an Encounter icon in the center or passes through an Edge with an Encounter Icon on it.  
After resolving the Encounter, Player may complete movement and transactions.  
   
For **Deck Encounters**: Player draws the top card from the corresponding Deck Type.  
For **Table Encounters**: Player rolls `1(R)` and consults the corresponding table.  
   
DECK ENCOUNTERS  
When a Deck Encounter is triggered, Player does the following:  
1) **Draw**. Reveal the top card of the corresponding deck.  
2) **Trigger Reaction**. Consult the posture bar in the middle of the card to see how the encountered character reacts to Player:  
i) **Identify Character’s Faction**. Indicated by the color of the posture bar, the faction gem icons, and the character bio.  
ii) **Calculate Player’s Favor (Uncapped)**. Count the number of positive Favor gems that Player has collected for the corresponding Faction and subtract the number of negated gems collected for that Faction (plus any temporary Favor modifier). **Faction Favor is uncapped** and can climb to `±5` or higher!  
iii) **Determine Character’s Reaction.** Compare Player’s Favor to the threshold compartments on the encounter card.  
iv) **Apply Reaction**. Possible reactions include:  
a) **Pistol Icon (`[pistol]`)**: Initiate combat immediately. *(Players cannot voluntarily initiate combat against non-threatening encounters unless unlocked by an active Combat Job or the ‘Doxxing’ Influence card.)*  
b) **`!!` (Threat)**: Player must fulfill the demand/job or initiate combat. If Player is only partially able to fulfill a demand, Player can avoid combat by satisfying the demand to the greatest extent possible (taking Hostile Favor consequences). If Player has none of the contraband being demanded (when noted on card), ignore the demand completely.  
c) **`!` (Demand)**: Player must fulfill the request/job or take Hostile Favor consequences (without combat).  
d) **`?` (Offer)**: Player may partake in the offered transaction or job (or decline with no penalty). This does not consume the Player’s hourly transaction for the turn.  
e) **Top Job Deck (`[computer][cash]`) vs. Bespoke Job (`[cash]`)**: `[computer][cash]` draws the top unseen card from the Jobs deck; `[cash]` assigns the bespoke job printed on the Encounter card.  

DECK TYPES  
**Criminals** – Street gangs, syndicates, hackers, and nocturnal fixers (`1–4×` icons).  
**Celebrities** – Tech founders, billionaires, politicians, and influencers (always `1×` icon).  
**Cops** – SFPD, DEA, ICE, ATF, CHP, and federal agents (`1–4×` icons).  
   
TABLE ENCOUNTERS  
**Protesters**:  
Roll `1(R)`:  
- `0`: Confiscates all of Player’s **Opiates**  
- `I`: Loots all of Player’s **Weed**  
- `II`: Seizes Player’s single most expensive Good  
- `III`: Clamors noisily (no loss)  

**Schizophrenic**: Player draws the top Job Card as a mandatory Schizophrenic Job and places `1(R)` Influence cards face down under it. Player collects those Influence cards when the job is satisfied. Face-down Schizophrenic cards count as “in Player’s hand” when calculating the price of buying Influence Cards in Clean-Up.  

**Open Mic (Stand-Up Comic)**:  
Roll `1(R)`:  
- `0` (**Roasted**): Undo every gem change from **Positive outcomes** (satisfying demands, accepting offers, buying/selling with NPCs, playing Influence cards, completing jobs)  
- `I` (**The Bit**): Undo every gem change from **Hostile outcomes** (refusing threats/demands, combat, being unable to pay, failing or abandoning jobs)  
- `II` (**Bombed**): Lose the rest of this turn and your next turn (flip SUV over)  
- `III` (**Killed**): Draw `1(R)` Influence Cards  

ADDICTION  
Every Dealer (player character) is a reprobate open mic comic and recovering stimulant addict.  

If a Dealer enters combat while in possession of **Uppers** (*Pill Uppers* or *Powder Uppers*), that Dealer will consume those Uppers and unleash the mandatory effects they have on combat, for better or for worse, whether or not the player desires for the Dealer to do so.  

Similarly, if the Dealer encounters an **Open Mic**, the Dealer will stop in for a set, whether or not the player desires for the Dealer to waste time or reputation to do so.

EDGES  
- **[Green]** Can pass through.  
- **[Red]** Cannot pass through.  
- **₿0.2** Pay ₿0.2 (or listed toll) to pass through.  
- **[Icon]** Resolve Encounter to pass through.  
   
STREET DICE GAMBLING  
If the Dice symbol is present on the revealed tile, Player can elect to spend their turn’s Transaction gambling:  
1) Player places a cash wager on top of the tile (**maximum ₿2.0 per gambling action**).  
2) Player rolls `1(R)` (or `2(R)` if *Match Fixing* is played).  
3) Player receives back Bitcoin equal to the total rolled times the bet:  
- `0`: **Lose** – Wager goes to the bank.  
- `I`: **Break even** – Wager is returned (`1×`).  
- `II`: **Double Up** – Receive `2×` wager (`+1×` net profit).  
- `III`: **Triple Up** – Receive `3×` wager (`+2×` net profit).  
*(The expected value of `1(R)` is 1.167×; with Match Fixing’s `2(R)`, up to a 6× payout is possible on the ₿2.0 maximum wager.)*  
   
**JOBS**  
A Player can have a **Maximum of five (5) active jobs**. There are three types of job assignments:

1) **Starting Jobs**. At the beginning of the game, Players receive five distinct jobs and pick three to keep. These jobs can be failed voluntarily (by canceling or replacing them when full). There is no Favor penalty for failing Starting Jobs *(unless **Surge Pricing** is active, which fines **-₿1.0** on every failed or unfinished job)*, **and Goods picked up for Starting Job delivery quests cannot be sold** (if abandoned, the cargo is discarded for nothing, never fenced).

2) **Deck Encounter Jobs**. A character encountered in San Francisco may give the Player a job. Goods picked up for Deck Encounter delivery jobs *can* be fenced to an interested buyer for the Retail Price (`+₿`) on the card, which immediately fails the job and triggers **Hostile Favor Consequences** from the patron *(unless **Clique Fence** is active, in which case fenced cargo sells **1 Scarcity step higher** and does **not** count as a Failed Job, though Patron Hostile Favor still applies)*. If a Deck Encounter Job is failed for any reason (including remaining unfinished at the end of Saturday!), Player receives the **Hostile Favor Consequences** from the patron.  

3) **Schizophrenic Jobs**. Mandatory and cannot be failed voluntarily (unless holding 6 Schizophrenic jobs simultaneously). Goods picked up for Schizophrenic delivery jobs cannot be sold, though they can be stolen.  

4) **Special Boss Combat Jobs (*Regime Change* & *Witness Protection*)**. Two high-stakes Jobs carry an **“any Player”** clause when **Donald Trump** or **Jared Kushner** is defeated in combat:  
- **Regime Change** (`mis_34`): Completed if **any Player** defeats **Donald Trump** or **Jared Kushner** in combat while you hold *Regime Change* (unless canceled by *Witness Protection*). Completing *Regime Change* **immediately ends the game in an instant victory** for the Player holding *Regime Change*!  
- **Witness Protection** (`mis_51`): Triggers **first** if **any Player** defeats **Donald Trump** or **Jared Kushner** in combat while you hold *Witness Protection*, awarding **₿2.0** (`20(d)`) and **canceling every active *Regime Change* job** across all Players while play continues normally!  

DEMANDS & COMBAT  
COMBAT  
1) Player takes (Hostile) Favor Consequences from the Encounter Card.  
2) Generate Player’s CP:  
- Player’s Driver has `1(R)` CP  
- `+` CP from Henchmen  
- `+` CP from Weapons *(Note: **Body Armor**—Stab Vest, Ballistic Helmet, Riot Shield, Ceramic Plate, Ballistic Vest—never adds CP; instead it absorbs wounds when you lose combat!)*  
- `+` Mandatory **Uppers** in SUV (must roll all Pill & Powder Uppers in combat!)  
- `+` `+1 CP` per positive MAGA Ruby if **Back the Blue** is active  
3) Opponent rolls their Combat Formula CP (`+2 CP` to all `Cops` NPCs if **Back the Blue** is active; *Distraction* deducts `1(R)` positive die from the target’s pool prior to rolling).  
4) All `(R)` on both sides are rolled simultaneously so post-roll Influence cards (*Twist of Fate*, *Pepper Spray*, *Backup Arrives*) may modify the dice.  
5) If **Player’s CP ≥ Opponent’s CP**: **Player wins**, collects Combat Loot (if any), and Player’s turn proceeds.  
Otherwise, **Opponent wins**:  
a) Player suffers `1` Wound (W) for each point of CP difference, reduced first by Body Armor / Henchman wound absorption (*“Destroyed when used”* armor such as Stab Vest and Ceramic Plate is only destroyed if needed to absorb at least 1 wound). Remaining wounds kill Henchmen first (Player chooses which Henchman dies if holding multiple; dead Henchmen return to the bottom of the Goods deck), and the Driver must survive if possible.  
b) Player’s turn ends.  
c) **If Player initiated combat by refusing a Threat (`!!`), Player must still fulfill the refused Threat** (to the greatest extent possible from remaining cash/goods/influence).  
   
DEATH (DEALER FATE)  
If an unblocked wound reaches the Driver, Player rolls `1(R)`:  
- `0`: **Yep, dead.** Player loses all Goods, ends turn; Player’s night is over.  
- `I`: **Left for dead.** Player loses all Goods, night continues.  
- `II`: **Beaten and mugged.** Player loses all Cash, night continues.  
- `III`: **Begged for mercy.** Player loses all Influence Cards, night continues.  
   
DAWN  
At the end of the 10th turn (5:00 A.M.), any Player whose SUV is still in San Francisco pays a sunrise curfew fine:
- **Thursday**: **₿1.2** (`₿0.6` on an Entry Point)  
- **Friday**: **₿2.0** (`₿1.0` on an Entry Point)  
- **Saturday**: **₿3.2** (`₿1.6` on an Entry Point)  

If the entire fine cannot be covered in Bitcoin, Player must pay the remainder in kind, forfeiting Goods with enough Uncommon (`=`) Retail Price (`+₿`) value to satisfy the amount owed.  

**BALLOT INITIATIVES & SUNDAY MORNING RECKONING**  
During Setup, Players draft active initiatives from the **6×3 Ballot Initiatives Board** (4 initiatives in a 2-Player game; `min(N, 6)` initiatives in 3–6 Player games; max 1 option per column):

| Column | Option 1 *(End-Game)* | Option 2 *(End-Game)* | Option 3 *(Mid-Game Initiative)* |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **MAGA (Ruby)** | **Mass Deportation**: *(Resolves FIRST)* **Least** MAGA favor is **ELIMINATED**! | **Recall Election**: **-₿1.0** per positive Ruby; **+₿0.5** per negative Ruby. | **Back the Blue**: All `Cops` NPCs gain **+2 CP**; each positive Ruby grants your SUV **+1 CP** in ALL combat. |
| **ISIS (Emerald)** | **Decapitation**: **Most** ISIS favor loses **-₿2.5**. | **Fatwa**: **+₿1.5** per positive Emerald if `≤ 0` Rubies, OR **-₿1.0** per positive Emerald if `≥ +1` Rubies; **-₿0.5** per negative Emerald. | **Sanctuary City**: When enacted, every Player immediately starts with a free Emerald (**+1 ISIS**). |
| **TECH (Diamond)** | **Series A**: **Most** TECH favor gains **+20%** of final wealth. | **Conflict of Interest**: **-₿1.5** per positive Diamond AND **-₿1.5** per negative Diamond. | **Lobbying Blitz**: Positive Diamonds may be exchanged **1-for-1** into Rubies (`+1 MAGA`) or Sapphires (`+1 DSA`) at any time. |
| **DSA (Sapphire)** | **Billionaire Tax**: **Most** DSA favor steals **₿1.5** from the wealthiest rival. | **Mutual Aid**: **+₿1.0** per net completed Job showing a `+Sapphire` reward OR issued by a DSA-aligned NPC. | **Rent Control**: When selling a Good (or Bulk Weed), spend positive Sapphires (**1 gem = +1 Scarcity step** toward `<<`). |
| **MS13 (Amethyst)** | **Protection Racket**: **Most** MS13 favor gains **+₿2.5**. | **Plata o Plomo**: **+₿1.0** per positive Amethyst AND **+₿1.0** per negative Amethyst. | **Clique Fence**: Fencing Encounter Job cargo sells **1 Scarcity step higher** and does **NOT** count as a Failed Job (Hostile Favor still applies). |
| **Gigs (Jobs)** | **Five-Star Courier**: **Most** net completed Jobs gains **+₿3.0**. | **Full Time Employee**: Receive **A × B ₿** (where `A` = your positive gems of one faction and `B` = net positive Jobs completed for that faction). | **Surge Pricing**: Completed Jobs pay **2× Bitcoin**, AND every failed or unfinished Job immediately fines **-₿1.0**. |

After Saturday Clean-Up concludes, **Sunday Morning Reckoning** arrives and each Player’s final score in **Bitcoin (`₿`)** is settled in strict order:
1) **Unfinished Jobs**: Any incomplete Jobs fail automatically (counting as Failed Jobs, fining **-₿1.0** each if *Surge Pricing* is active, and applying **Hostile Favor Consequences** for incomplete Deck Encounter Jobs).
2) **Unsold Goods Liquidation**: Remaining non-job Goods in a Player’s SUV liquidate at their **Abundant (`>>`) Retail Price (`+₿`)**.
3) **Unpaid Loans**: Deduct **₿4.5** for an unpaid Thursday Loan and **₿6.0** for an unpaid Friday Loan.
4) **Ballot Initiatives Settlement**:
   - **Step 4a — Elimination FIRST** (*Mass Deportation*, if active): The Player with **Least** MAGA Favor is **eliminated immediately** before any other Ballot Initiative awards or penalties resolve. Eliminated Players rank below all survivors, cannot win the game, and are **excluded from all subsequent Most/Least awards, penalties, steals, and percentage bonuses**!
   - **Step 4b — Flat Gem/Job Reckoning & Penalties**: Among surviving Players, resolve enacted flat per-gem/per-job modifiers and Most/Least penalties (*Recall Election*, *Decapitation*, *Fatwa*, *Conflict of Interest*, *Mutual Aid*, *Protection Racket*, *Plata o Plomo*, *Five-Star Courier*, *Full Time Employee*).
   - **Step 4c — Wealth Steal & Percentage Series A**: Among surviving Players, resolve *Billionaire Tax* (`₿1.5` stolen from the wealthiest surviving rival), followed by *Series A* (`+20%` of positive wealth).

The surviving Dealer with the highest final Bitcoin (`₿`) total wins!

FAVOR SUMMARY  
- **Uncapped**: Faction Favor has **no cap** (`±5` or higher is possible).  
- **(Ruby) – MAGA**: Heavily represented among the Cops (`11` of `30`); likes TECH, hates MS13, DSA, and ISIS.  
- **(Emerald) – ISIS**: Rarest faction (`14` cards total); poor (`0.6×` cash rewards), violent (attacks at `≤ 0`), hates everyone except MS13 and ISIS.  
- **(Diamond) – TECH**: Wealthy (`1.5×` cash rewards); split across Celebrities, Cops, and Criminals; ambivalent to MAGA, hostile to DSA.  
- **(Sapphire) – DSA**: Strongest among the Celebrities (`9` of `30`); hostile to MAGA and TECH.  
- **(Amethyst) – MS13**: Strongest among the Criminals (`7` of `29`); attacks at `≤ -1`, mostly indifferent to other factions.  
   
COMBAT BETWEEN PLAYERS  
**All ties in the game**—including every **Most** or **Least** Ballot Initiative award (even when Players are tied at `0`!), tied wealthiest rivals, and Final Score ties at Sunday Morning Reckoning—are broken by **Combat between the tied Players**, using Drivers (`1(R)`), Henchmen, Weapons, mandatory Uppers, combat Influence cards, and `+1 CP` per positive Ruby if *Back the Blue* is active.  
- **Pre-Roll vs. Post-Roll Combat Influence**: Pre-roll cards (*Drive-By* `+1(R)`, *Distraction* deducting `1(R)` positive die from the opponent’s pool prior to rolling) apply before dice are cast; all `(R)` on both sides roll simultaneously; and reactive post-roll cards (*Twist of Fate* rerolling a die; *Pepper Spray* zeroing an opponent’s highest die; *Backup Arrives* `+3 CP`) may be played when they can overturn a deficit or tie. *(In the digital edition, Players may opt out of auto-spending Combat Influence cards during Sunday Morning Reckoning tie-breakers during Saturday Clean-Up.)*  
- **Strictly One Recipient**: Every Most/Least bonus or penalty is awarded to **strictly one Player**. For **Most** awards (or *Decapitation*), the Combat **winner** claims the bonus (or escapes the penalty); for **Least** penalties or elimination, the Combat winner escapes and the Combat **loser** suffers the penalty!  
- **No Draws**: If a Combat between Players ends in a draw, repeat Combat (any one-shot Influence cards or consumable Weapons used in a previous round remain exhausted) until one side wins by at least **1 CP**!

## SYMBOL & ICON REFERENCE

### Goods & Henchmen Card Symbols
- **Bold Green `+₿` Retail (Sell / Fence Price Row)**: The top row on a Good Card’s price table. Indicates the **Bitcoin (`₿`) you receive** when selling that Good in San Francisco (or Fencing Encounter Job Cargo) at each Scarcity column (`<<` to `>>`).
- **Italic Red `-₿` Wholesale (Buy / Hire Price Row)**: The bottom row on a Good or Henchman Card’s price table. Indicates the **Bitcoin (`₿`) you pay** to purchase that Good at the Safe House Market (or an Open-Air Market) or to hire/retain a Henchman.
- **Bitcoin (`₿`)**: Currency (`1 Denarius = ₿0.1`) used for all purchases, sales, road tolls, Street Dice wagers, Henchman salaries, and final victory scoring.
- **`<<`  `<`  `=`  `>`  `>>` (5 Scarcity Columns)**:
  - `<<` **Precious** (`0` cards remaining on that category’s Safe House Market track)
  - `<` **Rare** (`1` card remaining)
  - `=` **Uncommon** (`2–3` cards remaining)
  - `>` **Common** (`4` cards remaining)
  - `>>` **Abundant** (`5–6` cards remaining)
- **`■` (Cargo Space Cubes)**: Squares indicating how many of your SUV Mat’s **6 Cargo blocks (`6■`)** a Good occupies (`1■`, `2■`, or `3■`).
- **`▲` (Henchman Seat Triangles)**: Triangles indicating how many of your SUV Mat’s **2 Henchman seats (`2▲`)** a hired Henchman occupies (`1▲` or `2▲`).

### San Francisco Tile, Action, & Encounter Icons
- **`(M)` (Blue Move Circle)**: Represents **1 SUV Movement** step along an open street edge.
- **`(T)` (Gold Transaction Circle)**: Represents **1 hourly Transaction** (Sell 1 Good, Bulk-Sell all Weed, Buy 1 Open-Air Market Good, or Gamble at Street Dice).
- **`(R)` / `®` (Action Die)**: 6-sided custom die with faces **`0, 0, I, I, II, III`** (average roll = `1.167`).
- **`++`  `+`  `=`  `-`  `--` (Tile Buyer & Open-Air Market Modifiers)**: Shifts the active Market Scarcity column 1 or 2 steps left toward `<<` Precious (`+` / `++`) or right toward `>>` Abundant (`-` / `--`).
- **Persistent (`⤹`) & Refresh (`↻` / `⤿`) Tile Icons**: Persistent landmark tiles remain face-up across nights; Refresh tiles discard after their encounter resolves and immediately reveal the next tile.
- **Encounter Icons**:
  - **Celebrities** (*Gold Hoodie Silhouette*): Draws 1 card from the Celebrities deck (`X = 1`).
  - **Cops** (*Blue 8-Point Police Hat Silhouette*): Draws 1 card from the Cops deck (`X = 1–4`).
  - **Criminals** (*White Tank-Top Silhouette*): Draws 1 card from the Criminals deck (`X = 1–4`).
  - **Protesters** (*Orange Megaphone*): Roll `1(R)` (`0`: lose Opiates; `I`: lose Weed; `II`: lose costliest Good; `III`: safe).
  - **Open Mic** (*Dark Gray Microphone*): Roll `1(R)` (`0`: undo Positive-outcome gem changes; `I`: undo Hostile-outcome gem changes; `II`: lose rest of turn & next turn; `III`: draw `1(R)` Influence).
  - **Schizophrenic** (*Purple A-Stance Silhouette*): Draws a mandatory Schizophrenic Job and seals `1(R)` face-down Influence cards beneath it until fulfilled.
  - **Street Dice**: Spend your hourly Transaction to wager `₿0.1–₿2.0` and roll `1(R)` to win `Wager × Roll`.
